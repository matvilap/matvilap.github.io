Is a piece for bass clarinet, percussion and electronics, performed by members of Ensemble Dal Niente. The piece reflects on life after the pandemic, which came to a halt in the real world but continued to unfold online in an accelerated manner. The piece blends digital instruments with processed analog recordings from the performers.
