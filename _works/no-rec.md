'No Recuerdo Como Era Antes' is a piece for bass clarinet, percussion and electronics. Performed by members of Ensemble Dal Niente, Kyle Flens (percussion) and Katherine Jimoh (bass clarinet). The piece reflects on life during the pandemic, which came to a halt in the real world but continued to unfold online in an accelerated manner. The piece blends digital instruments with processed analog recordings from the performers.
