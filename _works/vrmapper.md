VR-Mapper is a Max For Live device that can receive controller input and movement data. The data includes 3D position, euler rotation, velocity, acceleration and button/trigger input. The data is sent via OSC from a Unity scene. The device can combine, smooth, and scale data that can be mapped to other devices, tracks and effects in Ableton Live.
