*Unforeseen Collisions* is a networked VR system for musical collaboration. Two performers build audiovisual patterns in an emergent causality system. It was developed in Unity, using the Photon Unity Netowrking (PUN) to develop the networked virtual environment. The performers could join from remote locations to collaboratevliy perform an audiovisual composition. This performance has been presented under the title *Miskets & Canicas* at the 15th international symposium on Computer Music Multidisciplinary Research (CMMR) in Tokyo, the 18th Brazilian Symposium on Computer Music in Recife, and at the Digitalis Electronic Music Festival in Charlottesville.
